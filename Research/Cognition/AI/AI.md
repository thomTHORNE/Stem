# AI

The runtime every AI surface in Stem shares: which model answers, where it runs, and how consent works. Foundation → Ownership.

## Settled

- AI responds; it does not author. Its output always reads as a response.
- Nothing is sent to a cloud model without informed consent, and you know exactly what is sent.
- Configuration lives off the board, so the choice of model is not a board decision.

## Open questions

- **On device or cloud**, or both, and what each can and cannot do for recollection and conversation.
- **The consent flow.** How "exactly what is sent" is shown, and whether consent is per request, per board, or standing.
- **Conversation.** What talking a topic through looks like, where it lives relative to the board, and what happens to a conversation afterwards.
- **No AI configured.** Which surfaces degrade and which disappear.
