# Recollection

Finding a thought by asking for it in your own words, across everything put into Stem, with results ranked by meaning. Foundation → Recollecting instead of filing.

## Settled

- Nothing has to be named, tagged, or placed to be found.
- Everything is indexed: freeform drawing and writing, diagrams, markdown, cognition blocks, integrations.
- A result is the thought itself, shown where it lives. An AI may help reach it; it never stands in for it.
- References stay references in results.

## Open questions

- **Drawings.** Text is the tractable case. The most memorable thing on a board is usually the drawing, and finding a sketch by what it means is the hard one.
- **Ranking without a model.** What recollection does when no AI is configured or consented to — whether it degrades to text search or is unavailable.
- **The board grid.** It searches by title only. Whether recollection replaces that search, sits beside it, or is a separate surface.
- **Result shape.** What a result looks like when it is a stroke cluster, a block section, or a reference.

## Dependencies

- **Requires:** AI — matching by meaning needs a model. Cognition Blocks — blocks must expose their content to be indexed. GitHub — integration content is indexed and returned as reference.
