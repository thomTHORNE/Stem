# Meta Layer

What the layer over the board shows, who or what writes on it, and how it lets self-awareness develop without being handed conclusions. Foundation → Thinking about thinking.

## Settled

- It sits over the work like tracing paper and never changes the work beneath it.
- Nothing on it is presented as a conclusion about you.
- The ownership rules hold: anything Stem or an AI contributes reads as a response.
- Stem keeps what you keep. Deleting is your act, and deleted content is gone.

## Open questions

- **Who writes on it.** You, annotating your own work after the fact; Stem, recording the path as it happens; an AI, observing patterns; or some combination. Each produces a different product.
- **Whether history is kept.** Recording pivots and backtracks as they happen means persisting more than the board's current state. Keeping deleted content would change Foundation's "deleting is your act" from a statement about the board to one about the board's history.
- **What it shows by default**, and whether anything appears on it without being asked for.

## Findings

- **A question left unanswered is a signal that needs no AI.** In a cognition block where questions are posed and answered, one that is never answered marks a possible dead end. The signal comes from structure the user wrote, so it is compatible with Ownership by construction.

## Dependencies

- **Requires:** Cognition Blocks — block structure is the first known source of signal. AI — if an AI contributes to the layer.
- **Required by:** none.
