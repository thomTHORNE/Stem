# Origin

The brief that opened the conversation establishing Stem's pivot, 2026-10-18. Frozen.

---

This session focuses on establishing the pivot for the purpose of Stem, shifting it from being a mere extension of Notion to acting as a complete operating/cognition system, a digital mirror for the mind. The intention is deeply rooted in cognitive psychology and preserving human authenticity and ownership of one’s thoughts in the age of AI.

1. Embracing the "Messiness" of Human Cognition
Traditional tools force users to organize, file, and synthesize their thoughts into neat conclusions. Stem is intentionally designed to validate the ambiguity of the human mind.
   * Honoring Intuition: It encourages users to "follow your intuition" and capture "imperfect, unpolished thoughts" without the psychological friction of self-editing.
   * Validating the Struggle: By actively recording "a hunch," "a doubt," "a dead end," and the "pivot and backtrack," the app acknowledges that feeling lost or making mistakes is a vital part of the psychological journey toward understanding.
2. Meta-Cognition (Thinking About Thinking)
Stem is designed to foster self-awareness. It doesn't just store what you found; it provides a meta space on top of the base where you normally operate to explore how your mind works. Like a clear tracing paper when drawing, it adds a meta-lens to all your documented work through which you can gain a deeper understanding of yourself.
3. Recollecting instead of filing
A powerful search engine that indexes any kind of documented evidence (freeform drawing and text, diagram, markdown, an interactive cognition block, an integration) and feeds it to an AI of choice (on device or in cloud) that enables you to ask for something instead of listing keywords, resulting in a list of relevant results instead of results that are simply matched by a keyword.
4. An extensible platform that grows with you — a tool that is an extension of you
Stem provides a primitive mode, a canvas for freeform drawing and writing; it’s immediate and requires minimal effort encouraging users to transfer anything from their mind to a note. When an idea or a thought inevitably grows, so does its complexity and Stem is here to match that. Providing custom made interfaces called cognition blocks that are tailored to optimize efficiency for developing thoughts, ideas, learning, actions etc. They provide structure and a satisfying UI/UX that both aid in keeping the momentum going when things get complex.
Aside from cognition blocks, 3rd party integrations are also available. Currently only integration with github is aimed for v1 release where a custom interfacing block provides searching and navigating a repo, basic git commands like commit, pull and push, as well as full integration with Stem in form of annotating a github rendered document view and conversing with AI about the document.

Stem aims to remove the administrative anxiety of knowledge management. By providing functionalities like the powerful search engine, cognition blocks, instant freeform note taking and a meta-cognition layer, it frees up mental bandwidth and enables the user to just focus on "thinking naturally." It promises that things will "fall into place as you go," reducing the cognitive load on the user.
