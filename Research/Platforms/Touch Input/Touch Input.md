# Touch Input

How every capability of Stem is reached on a touch device without aiming.

## Settled

- Parity means the same capabilities, each through the input the device has. The input model follows what is attached: a hardware keyboard brings the keyboard model with it.
- "No aiming" holds on touch with more force than on a trackpad — a fingertip is larger than any handle Stem refuses to draw.
- No stylus pressure.

## Open questions

- **Modes.** Spring-loading is hold-a-key. Its touch equivalent, if any.
- **Connect.** Hint letters are typed. Connecting two objects by touch without aiming at either.
- **Tidy and snap.** `Tab` and `Shift` have no touch counterpart.
- **The insert menu.** `/` on a keyboard; how it opens on touch.
- **Draw versus pan.** One finger cannot both draw and move the canvas.
- **The on-screen keyboard** covering half the canvas while writing.
- **The principle itself.** Whether "Keyboard first" becomes a desktop rule under a broader principle that holds on both.
